## Description

An independent Mesen 2.1.1 reproduction of the public `elya-nes` ternary-transformer benchmark. The run produced 19/19 byte-identical generated tokens, measured 21,227,718 total CPU cycles and 1,117,248 mean cycles/token, with a 0-cycle difference from the published MAME mean.

Harness, raw result packet, source commit, and claim evidence are public and linked in `SOURCES.md`.
