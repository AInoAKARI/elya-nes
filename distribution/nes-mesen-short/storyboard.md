## Capture checklist

1. Record at 1080×1920 or crop desktop captures into a centered 9:16 safe area.
2. Keep repository text readable; zoom browser before recording rather than relying on post-production scaling.
3. Do not show local filesystem paths, browser account UI, tokens, cookies, private tabs, wallet secrets, or notifications.
4. Use no third-party music. A simple original click/pulse sound bed is optional.
5. Final duration must remain ≤60 seconds.
