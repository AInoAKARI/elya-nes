=== the +84 cycles on arm A, accounted for ==================================
The mixture branch changed the header-table sentinel from "advance to the next
bank" to "select this ABSOLUTE bank", because a mixture walk leaves the shared
region for an expert's banks and comes back, which an increment cannot say.
A dense build pays for that in two ways, both of which the merged tree inherits:

  header rows, main   1414   sentinels 6
  header rows, merged 1415   sentinels 7   (the walk now names its FIRST bank
                                            instead of assuming bank 0)
  cost   one extra sentinel body           71 cycles   (measured in situ on moe)
       + 2 cycles x 6 pre-existing switches 12 cycles
                                          --------
                                            83 cycles/token predicted
  measured  1,129,375 - 1,129,291 =         84 cycles/token   (+0.0074%)

Not a merge error; a merge COST, and it is the price of one header format
instead of two.
